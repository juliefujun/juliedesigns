CollegeBound App

CollegeBound App is a student success app that helps ease the transition from high school to college. Designed for both students and advisors, it offers features such as a calendar, journey map, and personalized resources to help students stay organized, informed, and confident throughout the college application process.

Approach

New college students often struggle to communicate with advisors and find important resources because information is scattered across multiple platforms and unfamiliar systems. CollegeBound App was designed to simplify this transition by centralizing advisor communication, planning tools, and essential resources in one intuitive experience. As the sole designer, I translated these goals into a clear visual framework that guided development and made the app approachable for first-time users.

Status

Currently this app is still under construction and being tested by students and advisors for improvement.

Key Learnings

This was my first flushed out prototype created on Figma and working with a team of software developers. It was insightful learning to collaborate with non-designers and see their perspectives and learned how important it is to get outside feedback.
