Sushigrams Concept

Sushigrams tests your ability to think quickly in this revolving sushi bar that's serving up letters you'll make into sushi. Sushigrams is a single player mobile puzzle game for ages 8 and up. It takes place in a revolving sushi bar where letters are sushi, turning preparation into a fast-paced word game. Letters constantly come and go on the conveyor belt, forcing players to manage them on their tables.

Approach

For this project, I worked with Alyssa Bobbit an Arts, Entertainment and Technology (AET) Major in a collaborative class called "Typography and Games". This project required a design sudent and an AET student to work as team. Our task was to develop a brand new game idea and design a typeface. We created an anagram style game that mimicked a revolving sushi bar because of our love for sushi!

My Role

As the design major, my role in ths project was designing a typeface and creating assets for our game. The Sushigrams typeface is inspired by Japanese-style woodcut letters and the assets found within the game reference the revolving sushi bar "Kura".

Key Learnings

This project challenged me to grow my illustrative skills. It was also insightful working on a team and learning how to make our ideas come to life through visuals.
