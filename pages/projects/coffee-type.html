This project explores typography in local coffee shops and its impact on branding, atmosphere, and user experience. I conducted a study focused on coffee shops near UT Austin. My goal was to understand and share how typography contributes to a coffee shop’s identity in a visually engaging publication.

Contents

Coffee + Type

My task was to write, design, print, and bind a publication in any area of interest. As a coffee enthusiast, I wanted to create a publication that explored the impacts of typography in coffee shop branding.

Process

For the content of my book, I focused on presenting findings from interviewing designers and coffee shop owners, photos I took when visiting each store, and specific typefaces used in each shop. My aim for the look and feel of the book was to create a coffee table like book. This came into fruition through an open, editorial style layout inside a hard cover case binding.

Key Findings

This project strengthened my understanding of typography’s role in branding and atmosphere and enhanced my hands-on design skills. I have a better understanding of layout and typographic design for publications, case binding and book production, ethnographic research, and managing deadlines.
