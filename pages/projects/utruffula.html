Utruffula Experience

Utruffula is an interface for mobile and web that creates a unique perception of plants aiming to deepen people’s connections to nature. Using a Lorax theme of caring for plants, the interface guides the user to different plants near the UT Art building. Each plant has a fact told in Lorax dialect to shape the headspace of the user.

Automation of a guided plant walk

For this project, I was tasked with creating an interface and experience that "cultivates a love of plants” near the UT Art Building. The class participated in plant walk guided by our professor. I thought about the joys of seeing an interesting, colorful plant which inspired the Lorax Theme. Moreover, the Lorax has a great care for his plants and environment aiding in the narrative of my design.​​​​​​​

My guiding question was how might I create a unique perception for my persona so that their newfound knowledge and interactions exceeds circumstance, deepening their connection to nature?

User Flow

Since I wanted to create an engaging experience for people to enjoy and learn, I created an interface that acted as a guided scavenger hunt.

  • Users are greeted with a brief instructional page
  • Using the map provided, users can explore plants around the art building
  • Once found and clicked on, a Lorax-Themed fact would appear to educate users about the specific plant
  • After reading, users can click a button that marks the plant as "seen" referencing a major symbol in the Lorax

Outcome

As my first coded website, this project challenged me to bring my designs from Figma into HTML and CSS. It took a lot of troubleshooting, but it gave me a greater appreciation for coding and left me with a website that reflects the ideas I set out to create.
