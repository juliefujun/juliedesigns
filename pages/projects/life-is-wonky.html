Life is Wonky

The goal of this project was to work with source material we observed and sketched, transforming others’ works into our own designs with new, meaningful messages that differ from the original form. I transformed a poster to create my own message. My design reflects how life rarely follows a neat, linear path, but within it you can find delight.

Working with constraints

I could not take photos of any posters that were presented to me in class to reference later, all elements I wanted to use were sketched and notated. The poster I took the most inspiration from was one that had elements blending into each other, influencing the visuals and messaging of my design.

Key Findings

Wrestling with an existing design and creating my own message was a unique challenge that helped me understand how to create intentional and meaningful design.
