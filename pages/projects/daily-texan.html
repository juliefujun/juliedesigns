The Daily Texan

At the the Daily Texan, I was on a team of reporters, copy editors, photographers, data analysts, comic artists, and other issue designers. Each role plays a part in creating a cohesive newsprint that contributes to the major, award-winning student newspaper with a daily circulation of ~12,000. It serves as a key source for campus news, sports, and, increasingly, digital content. As an Issue Designer, I contributed unique designs and graphics to enhance the visual elements of the many stories told in the Daily Texan.

A typical night of production

Designers are given stories to design layouts using brand assets and guidelines on Adobe InDesign. All designs go through three rounds of revisions. The first and second round designs are printed and checked for wording, spelling, and design errors. After getting your design approved by management editors, you make final changes for round three, but it not printed for review. Instead it is uploaded to a digital drive to later be combined, printed, and delivered across campus the following morning.

Constraints and challenges

The Daily Texan creates and delivers newsprints every Wednesday and Friday. All content and designs are done Tuesday and Thursday evenings and must be submitted for print by 12am the following day. This presents a challenge working on a large team to coordinate and meet deadlines. In order to overcome this, I had to practice being proactive and "pre-design" pages, or set up a typical layout, so when other teams submit stories, the design was already started. This aided in ensuring deadlines were met and The Daily Texan could deliver their newsprint seamlessly.

Growth opportunity

Layout design was completely new to be, but this taught me how to work with brand assets and create cohesive and visually engaging newsprints. If I were to go back to this role, I would expand illustrations and colors to expand stories visually and improve newsprint viewing used if permitted. However, for the amount of experience I had with layout design, I am happy with the work I provided and my ability to design under tight time constraints.
